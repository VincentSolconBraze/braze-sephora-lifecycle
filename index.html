The basics

Two building blocks behind every Braze identity

The entire system is built on two complementary identifiers.

🍪
device_id

The visitor cookie

On the very first visit to sephora.com or app open, Braze automatically generates a unique identifier stored in the browser (or app). This cookie persists across sessions and allows Braze to recognize an anonymous visitor on their return — exactly like a first-party cookie, fully under your control.

Lifespan: until the visitor clears their cache, switches browsers, or stays inactive for 12 months.

👤
external_id

The customer identity (your ID)

When a visitor logs in, your system passes their customer ID (e.g. their Sephora account ID) to Braze. This signal — the login — transforms an anonymous visitor into a named customer, merging the entire anonymous browsing history into their permanent profile.

Golden rule: never use an email address or phone number as the identifier. Always use an opaque internal ID.

What's coming

Probabilistic identity resolution

Roadmap 2025–2026

Identify your visitors… even without a login

Today, Braze can only link an anonymous visitor to a known customer at the moment of login (deterministic identification). Probabilistic resolution, planned on the Braze roadmap, will allow inferring the identity of an anonymous visitor from behavioral and contextual signals — without requiring them to log in.

Behavioral matching

Braze analyzes browsing patterns, products viewed, and visit timing to match an anonymous profile to a known customer with high confidence.

Cross-device signals

Visits from multiple devices sharing common signals (IP range, timezone, similar browsing profile) allow Braze to infer a likely shared identity.

Impact for Sephora

Personalized remarketing campaigns for non-logged-in visitors — lower acquisition costs and improved ROAS on paid channels.

GDPR note: Probabilistic resolution introduces important GDPR considerations: inferred data does not constitute explicit consent. Legal review will be required prior to deployment.
